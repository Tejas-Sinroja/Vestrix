## Do I need a commercial license?

You need one if the software is used **for, or on behalf of, a business**. For example:

- a developer uses it at work to understand, review or debug a company codebase, including internal-only code
- it's part of a paid service, consultancy engagement, product, or CI pipeline
- you ship it, or anything built from it, to customers
- a company offers it to its staff as a developer tool

You don't need one for personal side projects with no commercial intent, studying, or work at the noncommercial
organizations listed in the license.

If you're not sure, ask. We'll tell you plainly.

## What a commercial license gives you

- the right to use the software for any business purpose, for the seats or organization it covers
- terms suited to your use, such as per-developer, per-organization, or revenue-based pricing for products built with it
- optional support and early access to new features

The exact fee and terms are set in a written agreement between you and the licensor. The PolyForm license alone never
grants commercial rights.
